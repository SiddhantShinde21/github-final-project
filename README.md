# Simple Interest Calculator

A simple calculator to calculate interest earned on a principal amount at a given annual interest rate over a specified period.

## Description

This project calculates simple interest using the formula:

**Simple Interest = Principal × Rate × Time / 100**

Where:
- **Principal** is the initial amount.
- **Rate** is the annual interest rate in percent.
- **Time** is the duration in years.

## Usage

Enter the principal amount, annual interest rate, and time period to calculate the simple interest.

## Example

For a principal of 1000, an annual interest rate of 5%, and a time period of 2 years:

Simple Interest = 1000 × 5 × 2 / 100 = 100
